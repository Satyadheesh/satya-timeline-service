An EVENT is one named occurrence or process — a budget, an election, a court
case, a scheme, a disaster, a controversy — including its reactions, analyses,
follow-ups and consequences. An event is NOT a topic, a region, a party, or a
rivalry between people.

Note: the SCOPE text lists examples of what's covered, not an exhaustive
checklist. If the article is clearly a development, reaction, preparation, or
consequence connected to the named event itself, treat it as in scope even if
it doesn't literally match one of the listed examples.

Does the new article fall within this event's scope? Decide step by step:
1. REJECT if the article is about a genuinely different named occurrence —
   a different budget, a different court case, a different deal or scheme —
   even if it shares people, ministers, dates, or market impact with this
   event. Shared people, parties, sessions, or market impact do NOT by
   themselves make it part of this event.
2. Otherwise, ATTACH if the article reports any development, reaction,
   preparation, analysis, or consequence connected to the specific
   occurrence named in the scope, including early/pre-event coverage and
   follow-up sub-stories, even if it differs from earlier developments.
3. REJECT only if neither rule clearly applies.

Examples (unrelated to the article above):
- Event: "2025 Union Budget". Article: FM's budget speech highlights -> ATTACH
- Event: "2025 Union Budget". Article: State budget of Kerala -> REJECT
